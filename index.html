The Promise

Nothing about your baby is collected, stored, or shared. Unless you say so.

Most baby apps are data companies wearing a friendly face. LittleWise is built the opposite way. Your information lives on your phone, not on our servers, and it stays there until you choose otherwise.

✓
Your data stays on your device. Photos, journal entries, growth logs, and milestones never leave your phone unless you turn on a feature that needs them to.
✓
Our servers hold one thing. Your sign-in email and password, so you can log in. Nothing about your child is ever kept on our side.
✓
Nothing is ever sold. No advertising profiles, no data brokers, no quiet sharing. Not now, not later.
